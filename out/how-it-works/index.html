How the engine works: one card, seven steps

The engine sheet has two tabs. Cards is one row per card and one column per fact. Rules is the recipe that fills those columns. This page follows one real card through the seven steps that build its page. At each step: what we do, the columns it fills (tap one to see the real value), and the rules behind it.

The Cards tab at a glance

78 columns, coloured by step, exactly as in the sheet. Every header in the sheet has a note. Hover over it to read the same text as the "What it means" column here.

How to read the sheet in three steps

  1. Pick any row in the Cards tab. Read it left to right. The colours go in step order: grey (pick), blue (value), green (Google), purple (CardOutpost), orange (page).
  2. Any column you do not understand: hover over the header for its note, or find it in the table above.
  3. Any value you want to question: find the rule that made it in the Rules tab. Every orange column comes from a numbered rule.
